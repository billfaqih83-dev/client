Questions before the boxes?

A few helpful starting points for planning your move. Your final website can use your company's actual policies and service details here.

How far in advance should I plan a move?

As early as you can, especially for busy weekends or end-of-month dates. Share your preferred date so the moving team can discuss availability.

Can I get help with packing?

Packing support can vary by company and service package. Ask which packing options are available and what items need special handling.

What details help prepare an estimate?

Your moving date, origin and destination, home size, access details, and any special items help a mover understand the scope of the job.
