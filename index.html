Daily 2-hour operating plan

1
Research — 30 minutes
Find 5–10 relevant US businesses. Verify the website, actual business signals, decision-maker role, and direct LinkedIn URL. Record source links.
2
Qualify — 15 minutes
Score fit and urgency honestly. Choose one primary service based on evidence, not assumptions.
3
Personalize & contact — 35 minutes
Send a small number of thoughtful LinkedIn notes and emails. No scraping, bulk blasts, or automated invitations.
4
Follow-ups — 20 minutes
Check due dates, follow the schedule, and record every outcome. Stop on opt-out or negative response.
5
Improve — 20 minutes
Review replies and meetings, improve your message, and prepare for calls. Track results weekly.
