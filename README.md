# Client Acquisition OS

A lightweight, browser-based CRM to help a solo digital marketer acquire their first three clients through **manual LinkedIn and email outreach**.

## Run it
Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build step or paid service is required.

## Included
- Lead CRM with business, niche, decision-maker, email, website, direct LinkedIn URL, primary service, stage, priority, follow-up date, evidence/source, notes and last-contact outcome.
- Pipeline overview, reply rate, meetings, won-client goal and due follow-ups.
- CSV export and JSON backup.
- Editable LinkedIn/email/follow-up/discovery-call templates.
- A repeatable daily prospecting routine.

## Data and privacy
Lead data is stored in the browser's local storage on the device where you use the dashboard. It is not synced to GitHub or a server. Export JSON backups regularly. Avoid storing sensitive personal information.

## Responsible outreach
- Verify business signals and current decision-maker roles before contacting.
- Add source URLs and distinguish verified facts from hypotheses.
- Personalize messages and send them manually.
- Respect opt-outs and applicable email/privacy rules.
- Do not scrape LinkedIn or automate invitations.
- The dashboard does not send emails or LinkedIn messages.

## Suggested service priority
1. Meta Ads
2. Google Ads
3. Website development
4. Reputation management

Choose the primary service based on the prospect's verified situation rather than assuming a need.
